Privacy Policy

How we handle information when you visit this website.

Last updated: 28 September 2026

The short version

We do not ask you to create an account, we do not run our own analytics on you, and we do not sell or share your personal information. If you fill in the contact form or send us an email, we keep that message only so we can reply to you. If Google AdSense is displaying ads here, Google and its advertising partners may set cookies — that is covered in Section 6, and you can opt out at any time.

1. Who we are

This website is the official website of the Aiman & Minal YouTube channel. We are the publisher of this site. For any privacy question, write to us at business@aimankhan.com.

2. What we collect

DataWhenWhy
Your name, email and message Only if you submit the contact form or email us directly So we can reply to your business or collaboration inquiry
Channel statistics (subscribers, views, video counts) Automatically, at the time this site was built Read from the public YouTube Data API v3 and displayed for reference

That second row is worth reading carefully: the numbers on this site are ours, not yours. We query the public YouTube API and publish the result. Your browser never sends your identity to YouTube on our behalf, and we do not run any tracking script of our own.

3. What we do not collect

  • No account, no login, and no passwords.
  • No precise location, no device identifiers, no fingerprinting from us.
  • No behavioural profiles, and nothing sold or rented to anyone.

4. How long we keep it

Contact-form submissions and emails are kept only as long as needed to deal with your inquiry and to keep a record of any agreement that comes out of it. Once there is no ongoing matter, we delete them.

5. Embedded content from other platforms

Videos on this site are played using YouTube's own player. When you press play, YouTube receives the request directly from your browser and applies Google's Privacy Policy. We do not control what YouTube collects once that request is made. If you would rather not load third-party content, use the links to our social profiles instead of playing videos here.

6. Advertising and cookies

This site is funded by advertising. We use Google AdSense to display ads. Google and its advertising partners use cookies and similar technologies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites.

  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the internet.
  • You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • You can opt out of third-party vendor cookies more broadly at aboutads.info.
  • You can review and change your choices at any time through Google My Ad Center, or by clearing this site's cookies in your browser.

6.1 Your cookie choice on this site

The first time you visit, a banner asks how you want to be treated. Your answer is stored only in your own browser, using local storage on your device. It is not sent to us and we never see it. You have three options:

  • Accept all — ads are served, and they may be personalised using your activity on this and other sites.
  • Only essential — ads are served, but limited to what is needed to deliver them, so they are not personalised based on your activity.
  • Manage choices — we hand you to Google's consent manager, where you can change individual partner categories and withdraw your choice later.

Where you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, Google requires that this choice be made before personalised advertising cookies are set. That is why the banner appears before any advertising script loads.

If you do not answer, no choice is stored and the banner will ask again on your next visit.

6.2 Data we send to Google

When you accept, Google AdSense may receive technical information about the request, such as your IP address, browser type and version, the referring page, the operating system, and language. Where you have not opted in to personalised advertising, that information is not used to build a profile for you.

7. Children's privacy

This site is a general-audience site and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask us to show you the personal data we hold about you, to correct it, to delete it, or to object to certain uses of it. To make any of those requests, email business@aimankhan.com and we will respond.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page. Continued use of the site after a change means you accept the updated policy.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy: business@aimankhan.com.
