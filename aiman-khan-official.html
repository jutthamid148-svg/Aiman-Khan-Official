About the data on this site

Every number you see here was read from the public YouTube Data API. Nothing is estimated, rounded up for looks, or invented.

Where the numbers come from

Subscriber count, view counts, titles and upload dates were pulled from the YouTube Data API v3 on 28 September 2026 and then frozen into the page. The site makes no live API calls, so what you read is exactly what the channel reported on that date.

Why they can look different

YouTube updates these figures continuously and shortens counts in its own interface. A number here may therefore be slightly behind what you see on YouTube itself. The channel remains the source of truth.

What this site does not collect

No account, no sign-up and no server of our own. Your watch list is stored only in your own browser and is never sent anywhere. See the Privacy Policy for the full text.
